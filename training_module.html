Data Literacy Training Module

Build confidence in interpreting, communicating, and leveraging data for better decision-making.

01Module Overview

This three-week training module introduces educators to the foundations of data literacy. Each cohort will explore practical strategies for collecting, analyzing, and sharing student learning evidence in ways that inform instruction and foster collaborative problem-solving.

02Learning Objectives

  • Develop a shared vocabulary around data literacy and evidence-based instruction.
  • Practice using simple data tools to recognize trends, gaps, and outliers in student performance.
  • Design action plans that connect data insights to targeted instructional responses.
  • Establish routines for reflective dialogue and continuous improvement with grade-level teams.

03Session Schedule

Week 1 · Foundations

Explore core data literacy concepts, review ethical considerations, and examine sample data sets from classroom assessments.

Week 2 · Analysis Studio

Learn to create visual summaries, identify instructional priorities, and draft student support hypotheses in collaborative breakouts.

Week 3 · Action & Reflection

Prototype lesson adjustments, create implementation trackers, and plan peer feedback cycles to measure impact.
